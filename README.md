# Dragon Ball 3D Multiverse Portal 🐉⚡

An interactive, kid-friendly **3D Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball GT & Dragon Ball Super** learning and exploration portal featuring **12 languages** (including full Arabic RTL) and **Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD** Read Aloud narration.

🌐 **Live App**: [https://dragonball-3d-portal.web.app](https://dragonball-3d-portal.web.app)

---

## ✨ Features

- **🥋 25 Iconic Characters & 65+ Transformations**: Explore Son Goku (from Base, Super Saiyan 1–4, Flame-Red Super Saiyan God, and Super Saiyan Blue to **Ultra Instinct -Sign-** and **Silver-Haired Mastered Ultra Instinct**), Prince Vegeta (up to **Purple Ultra Ego**), Vegetto, Gogeta, Gohan Beast, Orange Piccolo, Broly, Freezer, Perfect Cell, Majin Buu, Beerus, Whis, Jiren, and Grand Zeno.
- **🎨 13-Color Saiyan Hair Guide**: Kid-friendly breakdown of what every Saiyan hair color means, how it is unlocked, and its power multiplier.
- **✨ 3D Capsule Corp Fusion Lab**: Compare Potara Earring fusions (Vegetto) vs. Metamoran Fusion Dance (Gogeta & Gotenks) with a live spiraling 3D convergence animation.
- **🌌 Complete 4-Era Timeline**: Follow the story chronologically across *Dragon Ball*, *Dragon Ball Z*, *Dragon Ball GT*, and *Dragon Ball Super*.
- **⚔️ Playable 1v1 Beam Struggle & 7-Star Shenron Quiz**: Pit any two characters and transformations against each other in an interactive Kamehameha tug-of-war, and collect all 7 Dragon Balls to summon Eternal Shenron.
- **☀️ Light & 🌙 Dark Themes**: Switch instantly between **Cosmic Night** (Dark Mode) and **Capsule Corp Daylight** (Light Mode), including matching 3D arena skies and WCAG AA high-contrast cards.
- **🌐 12 Languages + Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD Read Aloud**:
  - English (`en`), Español (`es`), 中文 普通话 (`zh`), 粵語 廣東話 (`yue`), 日本語 (`ja`), 한국어 (`ko`), Português (`pt`), Français (`fr`), Italiano (`it`), Deutsch (`de`), Nederlands (`nl`), and العربية (`ar` with full RTL layout).
  - Pre-rendered Studio **Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD** MP3 narration across all 12 languages on CDN, plus live multi-persona (`Aoede`, `Puck`, `Fenrir`, `Kore`, `Zephyr`, `Charon`, `Leda`, `Orus`) `/api/tts` synthesis when running locally.
