# Dragon Ball 3D Multiverse Portal 🐉⚡

An interactive, kid-friendly **3D Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball Daima, Dragon Ball GT & Dragon Ball Super** learning and exploration portal featuring **12 languages** (including full Arabic RTL) and **Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD** Read Aloud narration.

🌐 **Live App**: [https://dragonball-3d-portal.web.app](https://dragonball-3d-portal.web.app)

---

## ✨ Features

- **🥋 25 Iconic Characters & 75+ Canon Transformations**: Explore Son Goku (from **Kid Goku w/ Power Pole & Nimbus** and **Goku [Mini] Daima** through Super Saiyan 1–4, Flame-Red Super Saiyan God, Super Saiyan Blue, **Ultra Instinct -Sign-**, and **Silver-Haired Mastered Ultra Instinct**), Prince Vegeta (including **Royal-Blue SSB Evolved** & **Purple Ultra Ego**), Vegetto (Base, Gold SSJ1 & Vegetto Blue), Gogeta (including Crimson SSJ4 & Full-Power Blue), Son Goten, Kid Trunks, Gotenks, Gohan Beast, Orange Piccolo, DBS Broly, **Black Freezer**, Perfect Cell, Majin Buu, Beerus, Whis, Jiren, and Grand Zeno.
- **🔮 Canonical 1–7 Star Crystal Dragon Balls & Eternal Shenron**: Accurate manga star constellations (1–7 crimson stars in translucent amber-gold orbs, including Grandpa Gohan's 4-Star Ball) and full-body Eternal Shenron summoning.
- **🎨 13-Color Transformation Guide**: Kid-friendly breakdown of every Saiyan hair & divine/tyrant transformation color, how it is unlocked, and Official Daizenshuu vs. Estimated power benchmarks.
- **✨ 3D Capsule Corp Fusion Lab**: Compare Potara Earring fusions (Vegetto) vs. Metamoran Fusion Dance (Gogeta & **Goten + Trunks = SSJ3 Gotenks**) with a live spiraling 3D convergence animation.
- **🌌 Complete 5-Era Timeline**: Follow the story chronologically across *Dragon Ball*, *Dragon Ball Z*, *Dragon Ball Daima* (Akira Toriyama's Demon Realm adventure), *Dragon Ball GT*, and *Dragon Ball Super*.
- **🎮 Interactive 3D Stage Toybox & Hidden Dragon Ball Radar Hunt**:
  - **⚡ Hold-or-Tap Ki Limit-Break**: Charge `0% → 100%` Ki to scale the 3D aura & floating rocks — reaching `100%` triggers an automatic **Limit-Break Evolution** into the fighter's next canon transformation!
  - **☄️ Signature Ultimate Cannon**: Fire each character's canon signature attack (*Kamehameha!*, *Galick Gun!*, *Death Ball!*, *Special Beam Cannon!*, etc.) across the 3D arena with a glowing scorched crater impact.
  - **💨 Zanzōken / Instant Transmission**: Tap anywhere on the 3D arena or press Teleport to warp with Akira Toriyama horizontal speed-scanline afterimages.
  - **🔮 3D Dragon Ball Radar Hunt**: Lock onto hidden 1–7 Star Crystal Dragon Balls around the 3D arena perimeter and tap to collect them toward summoning Eternal Shenron!
- **🧭 Kid-First 6-Mode Navigation & Sticky Mobile Bottom Bar**: Dedicated top-level **⚔️ VS Battle** and **🐉 Shenron Quiz (`0/7 ★`)** modes, thumb-friendly 5-tab mobile bottom app bar (`🥋 Heroes`, `✨ Fuse`, `⚔️ Battle`, `🐉 Quiz`, `📚 Lore`), stage `◀ / 🎲 / ▶` quick-stepper, and one-tap Character Card action chips.
- **⚔️ Playable 1v1 Beam Struggle & 7-Star Shenron Quiz**: Pit any two characters and transformations against each other in an interactive Kamehameha tug-of-war, and collect all 7 Dragon Balls across trivia challenges & 3D Radar Hunts to summon Eternal Shenron.
- **☀️ Light & 🌙 Dark Themes**: Switch instantly between **Cosmic Night** (Dark Mode) and **Capsule Corp Daylight** (Light Mode), including matching 3D arena skies and WCAG AA high-contrast cards.
- **🌐 12 Languages + Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD Read Aloud**:
  - English (`en`), Español (`es`), 中文 普通话 (`zh`), 粵語 廣東話 (`yue`), 日本語 (`ja`), 한국어 (`ko`), Português (`pt`), Français (`fr`), Italiano (`it`), Deutsch (`de`), Nederlands (`nl`), and العربية (`ar` with full RTL layout).
  - Pre-rendered Studio **Google Cloud Chirp 3 HD** MP3 narration across all 12 languages on CDN, plus live multi-persona (`Aoede`, `Puck`, `Fenrir`, `Kore`, `Zephyr`, `Charon`, `Leda`, `Orus`) `/api/tts` synthesis when running locally.
